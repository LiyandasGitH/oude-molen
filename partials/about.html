ABOUT OUDE MOLEN

Established in 1961 as the first Technical High School in the pre-democratic Cape Province, Oude Molen became in 2008 the first technical high school to be awarded Dinaledi School status — a mark of excellence in Mathematics and Science teaching.

We serve roughly 820 energetic learners and focus on mathematics, science and technology through the medium of English. We run on the principles that make Non-Profit Organisations successful, with a fully qualified professional academic staff.

Vision Statement
"We are committed to the holistic development of responsible citizens with rewarding careers in innovative technology."
Mission Statement
  • Providing high school learners with a balanced education as a preparation for careers in science and technology
  • Developing acquisition of skills in communication, mathematical sciences and technical fields of study
  • Encouraging physical and social development through sport, cultural activities and life orientation
